## About the course

This course provides an in-depth understanding of semiconductor device physics and the fundamentals of device fabrication. You learn the essential concepts of semiconductor processes, from the basics of device physics to manufacturing process flows, including layout and design rules.

The second part of the course focuses on process integration challenges and their impact on yield, performance and reliability. You explore key issues in device performance and reliability and learn how to determine appropriate follow-up actions for process integration problems.

## Learning outcomes

- **LO1:** Identify semiconductor manufacturing process flows based on device physics.
- **LO2:** Determine follow-up actions for process integration issues and performance.

## Topics covered

**Topic 1 – Fundamentals of Device Physics and Device Fabrication**

- Semiconductor materials and carrier physics
- PN junctions and diode behaviour
- MOSFET and BJT device operation
- Memory devices and integrated circuits
- Wafer fabrication process modules (oxidation, RTP, lithography, CMP, etch, doping, deposition, metallisation)
- CMOS integration flows
- Layout and design rules

**Topic 2 – Process Integration Issues**

- Isolation, wells and self-aligned modules
- Contamination, yield and process variation
- Device performance characterisation
- Device reliability and failure mechanisms
- Assembly, packaging and final test
- Optimisation and emerging technologies
